# Customer Success Manager

## About us

We help independent cafés manage bookings, stock and staff rotas. Most of
our customers run small teams on tight margins, so the help we give them
matters.

School pick-ups, a parent to look after, a hospital appointment? We do too.
Core hours are 10 to 3, and you fit the rest around your life.

## What you'll do

You'll look after about 150 cafés, from their first week with us to their
renewal. You'll run onboarding and training, spot customers who are
struggling before they tell us, and bring what you learn back to the
product team.

## What matters most

1. You care. In this job that means following up with a café owner when
   nobody asked you to, and noticing when a colleague is stuck.
2. You've looked after customers of a software product before.
3. You can explain something technical to someone who's busy and tired.

## Nice to have

- HubSpot or Zendesk
- Running renewals
- Experience in hospitality

## The offer

- 25 days' holiday plus bank holidays
- Pension
- A learning budget of £1,000 a year
- Tell us what would help you at interview or at work, and we'll sort it
