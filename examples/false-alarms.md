# IT Support Analyst

Salary: £28,000 to £32,000. The range is the same for everyone at this level.

## What you'll do

Join our customer support team at an NHS Foundation Trust. You'll look
after Active Directory, respond to tickets, and work through the
responsibilities below with a team lead who's been doing this for years.

## Responsibilities

- Respond to support tickets within agreed times
- Manage user accounts in Active Directory and SharePoint
- Help with lead generation reporting for the fundraising team
- Keep our analytics dashboards up to date

## Benefits

- Competitive pension and share options
- Childcare vouchers
- Feel free to ask about flexible hours: most of the team work them
