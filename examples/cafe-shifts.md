# Café Team Member

The Old Mill Café is looking for a young, energetic team player to join our
busy café in the heart of town.

## About the role

You'll serve customers, make coffee, clear tables and help in the kitchen
when it's busy. Hours vary from week to week.

## What we need

- Must be flexible to work any shifts, including early mornings, evenings
  and bank holidays
- Weekends essential
- Barista experience
- Food hygiene certificate
- Able to stand for long periods and carry heavy deliveries
- Own transport, as we're not on a bus route
- A bubbly personality

## What we offer

- £11.44 an hour
- Staff discount and free drinks on shift
- Tips shared weekly
