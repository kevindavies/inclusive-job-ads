# Review: Customer Success Manager, before and after

Same role, same company, so everything is comparable.

What got better

- Flexibility moved from one line at the bottom to the second paragraph, and became specific ("Core hours are 10 to 3") and shown in use ("School pick-ups, a parent to look after, a hospital appointment? We do too."). Evidence: field for flexibility; untested as wording.
- Twelve must-haves became three essentials plus a "Nice to have" list. The degree and the driving licence are gone. Evidence: field for volume; lab for the disability point.
- The person line ("a confident, ambitious Customer Success Manager") became what the job does, and "You care" is made concrete: following up when nobody asked, noticing a colleague who's stuck. A candidate can now show it.
- An offer of adjustments appeared. Evidence: lab, not tested on real applications.
- A learning budget with a figure (£1,000 a year) replaced "Team socials every month", which suits more people.
- Plain language throughout, and the description of the day-to-day is much clearer.

What got worse

- The salary has gone entirely. The old ad had two conflicting ranges; the new one has none, which is a bigger problem for the readers who most need it. Evidence: field.
- Still no word on whether pay is negotiable. Evidence: field.
- The ad no longer says where the job is based or how many days, if any, are in an office. "Fit the rest around your life" is warm, but a reader can't plan around it.

Still to fix

1. Put back one pay range near the top, and say whether it's negotiable. Evidence: field. For example: "[£X to £Y], depending on experience. Pay is negotiable within the range."
2. Say where the job is based and how much of it is remote, in one line next to the core hours. Evidence: field (specific flexibility).
3. Make sure the "We do too" line is true and visible: say who on the team does the school run, or what the team actually does about it. Evidence: guidance; my view, and no study has tested this wording.

Word check: before → after

Script-adjusted: 0 communal, 7 agentic → 0 communal, 0 agentic. After my read: about 3 agentic worth changing → none. Pay figures: two conflicting ranges → none.
