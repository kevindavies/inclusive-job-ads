# Office Coordinator

We're a warm, supportive and collaborative team, committed to
understanding each other and working together. We're looking for a
young, energetic recent graduate to join us.

- To be fit and active, as you'll be on your feet a lot
- A full driving licence
- To be a digital native, comfortable with all our systems
- Confidence and independence
- A competitive, determined attitude

## Pay

Competitive, depending on experience.

He or she will report to our chairman.
