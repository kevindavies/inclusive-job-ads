# Part-time Bookkeeper

£15,600 to £18,000 a year for three days a week. The top of the range is
negotiable.

You choose which three days. Most of the work fits school hours, and you can
work from home on two of them.

## What you'll do

You'll keep the books for a family-run garden centre with 14 staff:
suppliers' invoices, the weekly payroll, the VAT return each quarter, and a
short monthly report for the owners.

## What you'll need

- You've kept the books for a small business, including payroll
- You're comfortable with Xero or a similar package
- You can explain a set of numbers to someone who isn't an accountant

## Nice to have

- AAT or a similar qualification
- Experience in retail

## Working here

When the tills went down last Christmas, the owners were on the shop floor
with a cash tin. Nobody here thinks a job is beneath them.

## Applying

Send a CV, or just a few lines about what you've done. Tell us what would
help you at interview or at work, and we'll sort it.
