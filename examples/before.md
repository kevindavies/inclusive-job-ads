# Customer Success Manager

Salary: £32k to £38k depending on experience

## About us

We're a fast-growing software company helping independent cafés manage
bookings, stock and staff rotas. We're ambitious, and we're proud of our
4.8 rating on Trustpilot.

## Who we're looking for

A confident, ambitious Customer Success Manager who will champion our
customers and drive retention across a portfolio of 150 accounts. You'll be
the decisive voice of the customer in product decisions.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in customer success or account management
- Experience with SaaS products
- Experience with HubSpot and Zendesk
- Excellent written and verbal communication
- Strong analytical skills
- Commercial awareness
- Experience running onboarding and training sessions
- Experience managing renewals and upsells
- Confident presenting to senior stakeholders
- Able to manage competing priorities
- A degree in business or a related subject
- Full UK driving licence

## The offer

- An attractive salary of £35k to £42k
- 25 days' holiday plus bank holidays
- Pension
- Flexible working
- Team socials every month
