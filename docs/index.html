A Claude skill that writes job ads, and reviews them the way a kind colleague would.

An ad rarely says “you can’t do this”. Too often it says “you won’t fit in”. The skill finds those lines, and the ones that tell people they’d belong, then suggests specific fixes.

Every check is grounded in published research, and says how strong that research is. It’s built to support inclusive hiring practices like those used in B Lab’s standards for B Corps. It’s free and open source.

What it checks

  1. Pay field evidence
  2. Flexibility field evidence
  3. Age wording field evidence
  4. Disability and adjustments lab, legal
  5. Requirement lists mixed
  6. Gendered and agentic words contested
  7. Culture, perks and caring guidance

Strongest evidence first.

Install

git clone https://github.com/kevindavies/inclusive-job-ads.gitcp -r inclusive-job-ads/inclusive-job-ads ~/.claude/skills/

Then ask Claude to write a job ad, or paste one and ask for a review.

For any size of employer

A founder’s first hire, a B Corp, or a global employer with a library of templates. Fix one template and every ad built on it improves.
