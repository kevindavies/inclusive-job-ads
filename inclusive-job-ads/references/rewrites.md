# Rewrites

Read this before suggesting changes. The pattern: describe what the job does,
not what the person is. Keep the meaning. Use placeholders for facts you
don't have. Check your rewrite doesn't add new trait words of its own.

## Pay

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Competitive salary | `[£X to £Y]`, depending on experience. Not sure where you'd sit? Ask us |
| Salary DOE | `[£X to £Y]`. We'll tell you where you'd start before you interview |
| Two different figures in one ad | One range, stated once near the top |
| (no mention of negotiation) | Pay is negotiable within the range / The range is fixed for everyone at this level |

## Flexibility

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Flexible working (in a perks list) | Near the top: "Core hours are 10 to 3. Fit the rest around your life." |
| Hybrid | `[N]` days in `[place]`, the rest wherever suits you |
| Family-friendly | School pick-ups, a parent to look after, a hospital appointment? We do too. |

## Age

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Recent graduate | Early in your career / No prior experience needed |
| Young, dynamic team | A friendly team that `[what the team does]` |
| Digital native | Comfortable with `[the actual tools]` |
| 10+ years' experience | Experience of `[the thing those years were meant to prove]` |

## Disability

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Must be active / fit | Name the task: "Spend about half the day on your feet in the warehouse" |
| Driving licence required (desk job) | Remove it, or say why the job needs one |
| (nothing on adjustments) | Tell us what would help you at interview or at work, and we'll sort it |

## Requirements

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Twelve must-haves | Three to five essentials, then "Nice to have" for the rest |
| Degree from a top university | The skill the degree stood for |
| Must have 8+ years | Experience building `[the thing]` at `[the scale]` |

## Gendered terms

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| He will report to | You'll report to |
| He/she, s/he | You, or they |
| Chairman, salesman, foreman | Chair, salesperson, supervisor |
| Guys | Everyone, team, folks |

## Agentic trait words

Only change trait words the job doesn't need.

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| A driven, competitive self-starter | Someone who enjoys setting their own goals and seeing them through |
| Confident, ambitious | Ready to take on `[the responsibility]` and grow into `[the next step]` |
| Lead the charge on our platform | Shape and look after our core platform |
| Defend your opinions | Make your case, and change your mind when the evidence does |
| Dominate the market | Become the first choice for `[customers]` |
| Champion an analytical approach | Use data to test what works |

Keep "lead" when the job is leading: "Lead a team of four" is the job.

## Caring and values

Make values concrete, so a candidate can show them.

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| You care | You follow up with a customer when nobody asked you to. You notice when a colleague is stuck and offer help |
| We value diversity | What you actually do: "We publish our pay bands", "Interviews are structured and scored the same way for everyone" |
| Work hard, play hard | What a normal week looks like |
| Beer fridge and ping-pong | Perks that work for more people: `[learning budget, flexible hours, decent leave]` |
